I find myself thinking
about one of the physics problems
I encountered in school.
Let me try to put it in a simple context.
Imagine you are a hungry crow,
and you see a truck full off delicious food.
You start flying towards the truck at your maximum speed (v),
while the truck,
moves on a straight highway at a much slower pace (u).
If right now you and the truck move perpendicular to eachother,
when will you reach your lunch,
given some distance (l) between you?
